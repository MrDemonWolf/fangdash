<Callout type="info">
	**TL;DR** — Sign in with Twitch to save your progress and race others. FangDash stores your Twitch
	username, avatar, Twitch ID, and email — nothing more. Share a room code so your chat can race
	you, up to 4 players at once.
</Callout>

FangDash is built for **Twitch streamers** and their communities. You can play solo with no account at all, but signing in with Twitch is what saves your scores, XP, achievements, and skins — and unlocks multiplayer racing.

## Signing In With Twitch

Twitch is the **only** way to sign in. Authentication runs on [Better Auth](https://www.better-auth.com/) with Twitch OAuth, and sessions are cookie-based with a short 5-minute cookie cache.

<Steps>
	<Step>Click **Sign in with Twitch** on the FangDash site.</Step>
	<Step>Approve the connection on Twitch's authorization screen.</Step>
	<Step>You're returned to FangDash, signed in and ready to save scores and race.</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
	Want to try the game first? You don't need an account for solo play at `/play`. Sign in only when
	you want to save progress, appear on the leaderboard, or race live.
</Callout>

## What Twitch Data Is Stored

When you sign in, FangDash stores just enough of your Twitch profile to identify you and show your name on leaderboards and races:

| Data            | Why it's stored                                          |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Twitch username | Shown on your profile, leaderboards, and race results    |
| Twitch avatar   | Shown next to your name in-game                          |
| Twitch ID       | Stable identifier that links your account to your scores |
| Email address   | Account identity and verification (stored, never shown)  |

That's the full list. FangDash does **not** store your followers, subscriptions, chat, or any other channel data. For the complete details on what's collected, how long it's kept, and your rights, read the [Privacy Policy](/docs/legal/privacy-policy).

## Racing Your Chat

As a streamer, create a race room and share the room code with your chat. Viewers join in three steps:

<Steps>
	<Step>Open the FangDash website.</Step>
	<Step>Sign in with their Twitch account.</Step>
	<Step>Enter your room code to join the race.</Step>
</Steps>

Up to **4 players** can race at once. A race starts as soon as at least **2 players** are ready, followed by a **3-second countdown**. Everyone runs the exact same procedurally generated course from a shared seed, so it's fair by design — see [How Multiplayer Works](/docs/multiplayer/how-it-works) for the technical details.

## Stream-Friendly Features

- **Wolf skins** are visible to every racer, so viewers can show off what they've unlocked.
- **Leaderboards** filter by difficulty and by day, week, or all-time — great for an on-screen overlay.
- **Race results** list each player's name and score at the finish, with bonus XP for the top 3 placements.

## Your Data, Your Control

You decide how visible you are. In [Settings](/docs/getting-started/settings) you can toggle your profile between **public** and **private** — private profiles are hidden from the public leaderboard. You can also delete your account entirely, with a **24-hour grace period** before the data is permanently removed. Full details are in the [Privacy Policy](/docs/legal/privacy-policy).

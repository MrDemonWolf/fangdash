<Callout type="info">
	**TL;DR** — Equip skins from the Settings page (moved from the Skins gallery), toggle profile
	privacy to control who can see your public profile, and request account deletion with a 24-hour
	grace period to cancel before the data is removed.
</Callout>

The Settings page at [/settings](/settings) is your central hub for managing your FangDash account. You'll need to be [signed in with Twitch](/getting-started/account) to reach it.

## Skin picker

Equipping skins now lives on the Settings page instead of the Skins gallery. Browse the wolves you've unlocked and select one to equip. Locked skins stay visible with their unlock conditions listed, so you always know what to chase next.

Head to the [Skins gallery](/skins) to preview every wolf, then come back to Settings to equip the one you want.

## Privacy toggle

Control whether other players can view your public profile.

| Setting     | Behavior                                                                  |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Public**  | Your profile is visible at `/profile/[id]` and linked from leaderboards   |
| **Private** | Your profile page returns a "private" notice; leaderboards hide your link |

The toggle is labeled **"Allow others to view my profile"** and defaults to public.

## Account deletion

<Callout type="warn">
	Account deletion is **scheduled** after the grace period, not instant. Once the deletion runs, all
	of your scores, achievements, skins, and race history are removed.
</Callout>

FangDash supports GDPR-compliant account deletion with a built-in safety net:

<Steps>
	<Step>Click **Delete Account** on the Settings page.</Step>
	<Step>Confirm the deletion request.</Step>
	<Step>A **24-hour grace period** begins — your account is soft-deleted but not yet removed.</Step>
	<Step>
		During the grace period, return to Settings and click **Cancel Deletion** to restore your
		account, no data lost.
	</Step>
	<Step>
		After 24 hours, the account becomes eligible for permanent removal and stays in a soft-deleted
		state until the hard-delete process runs.
	</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
	Permanent data removal is not yet automated. After the grace period expires, an operator runs the
	hard-delete process. A scheduled Cloudflare Worker cron is planned to automate this step in a
	future release.
</Callout>

### Account API endpoints

The account management endpoints live in the `account` tRPC router:

| Endpoint           | Type     | Description                                                     |
| ------------------ | -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `getAccountStatus` | Query    | Returns the current deletion status and timestamps              |
| `requestDeletion`  | Mutation | Schedules the account for deletion after a 24-hour grace period |
| `cancelDeletion`   | Mutation | Cancels a pending deletion request                              |
| `getPrivacy`       | Query    | Returns the current privacy setting                             |
| `updatePrivacy`    | Mutation | Toggles profile visibility on or off                            |

### Database schema

Two fields on the `user` table back account deletion:

- `deletionRequestedAt` — timestamp when you requested deletion
- `deletionScheduledFor` — timestamp after which the account is eligible for permanent removal (24 hours after the request)

The `player` table carries a `profilePublic` boolean field for the privacy toggle.

<Callout type="info">
	**TL;DR** — 11 pages: Home, Play, Race, Leaderboard, Achievements, Skins, Settings, Profile,
	Public Profile, Changelog, and Admin. You can browse and play solo without an account; signing in
	with Twitch saves your progress and unlocks multiplayer. Admin is restricted to admin/dev roles.
</Callout>

Here's a quick tour of every page in the FangDash web app.

## Pages

| Page               | Route           | Description                                                           |
| ------------------ | --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Home**           | `/`             | Landing page with a quick overview and a sign-in prompt               |
| **Play**           | `/play`         | Start a solo endless runner game                                      |
| **Race**           | `/race`         | Create or join a multiplayer race room (rooms live at `/race/[code]`) |
| **Leaderboard**    | `/leaderboard`  | Global rankings with daily/weekly/all-time and per-difficulty filters |
| **Achievements**   | `/achievements` | View all 21 achievements and your unlock progress                     |
| **Skins**          | `/skins`        | Gallery of all 6 wolf skins and their unlock conditions               |
| **Settings**       | `/settings`     | Equip skins, toggle profile privacy, and delete your account          |
| **Profile**        | `/profile`      | Your stats, level, recent scores, and equipped skin                   |
| **Public Profile** | `/profile/[id]` | View another player's public profile (linked from the leaderboard)    |
| **Changelog**      | `/changelog`    | Release notes and version history                                     |
| **Admin**          | `/admin`        | Player, score, and race management (admin/dev roles only)             |

The **Admin** area also has focused sub-pages: `/admin/players`, `/admin/scores`, and `/admin/races`. Two utility routes round things out — `/offline` (shown by the PWA service worker when you lose connection) and `/auth/error` (sign-in error handling).

## Getting Around

The main navigation links to Play, Race, Leaderboard, Achievements, and Skins. Your profile and settings live in the user menu in the top-right corner once you're signed in.

## What Requires Sign-In

You can jump into a solo game and browse most of the site without an account — but your run won't be saved. **Sign in with Twitch** to:

- Save your scores, XP, achievements, and skin unlocks
- Join multiplayer races
- Equip skins (via Settings)
- View your own profile and manage account settings

The **Home** page, **Leaderboard**, **Achievements** list, **Skins** gallery, **Changelog**, and **public profiles** are all viewable without signing in — public profiles are shown only when the player has kept their profile public.

<Callout type="info">
	FangDash signs you in with Twitch and stores your Twitch username, avatar, Twitch ID, and email.
	See [Accounts & Privacy](/docs/getting-started/settings) for what's kept and how to delete it.
</Callout>

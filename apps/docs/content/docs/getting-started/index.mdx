<Callout type="info">
	**TL;DR** — Sign in with Twitch (no password needed), hit **Play** for an instant solo run, then
	head to **Race** to compete with up to 3 other wolves.
</Callout>

Welcome to FangDash, the free, open-source, browser-based endless runner for Twitch streamers and their communities. You control a wolf that runs forward on its own — your job is to jump, dodge procedurally generated obstacles, and survive as long as you can while climbing the leaderboard.

You can play solo right now without an account. Signing in with Twitch is what saves your scores and XP, unlocks skins and achievements, and lets you race other players in real time.

<Steps>
<Step>
## Sign in with Twitch

Click **Sign In** in the top right and authorize FangDash. Twitch is the only sign-in method, so there's no password to create.

When you connect your account, FangDash stores your Twitch **username**, **avatar**, **Twitch ID**, and **email address** — that's the full scope of the profile data it keeps. See the [privacy policy](/docs/legal/privacy-policy) for exactly what's collected and how it's used.

<Callout type="info">Want to try before signing in? Solo play works without an account — you just won't save scores, XP, or unlocks.</Callout>
</Step>

<Step>
## Play your first game

Click **Play** to start a solo run. Your wolf runs forward automatically, so you only ever control the jump: press **Space**, or tap/click anywhere. You get a **double jump** (up to two jumps before landing), and **holding** jumps higher than a quick tap.

Dodge the rocks, logs, bushes, and spikes for as long as you can. The further you run, the faster and harder it gets. Full move list is in [Controls](/docs/gameplay/controls).

</Step>

<Step>
## Check your score

When your run ends, your score is saved automatically (if you're signed in). You earn **10 points per second** survived plus **50 points** for each obstacle you clear — and every point is also **1 XP** toward your [level](/docs/gameplay/levels).

Open the [Leaderboard](/docs/gameplay/leaderboard) to see how you stack up. You can filter by difficulty and by daily, weekly, or all-time periods.

</Step>

<Step>
## Join a race

Head to **Race** to create or join a room and compete with up to **3 other players** (4 wolves total). Once at least two players are ready, a 3-second countdown kicks off and everyone runs the **same procedurally generated course** from one shared seed — so it's fair by design.

Rival "ghosts" show you where the other wolves are. Finish in the top 3 to earn bonus XP: **500** for 1st, **250** for 2nd, and **100** for 3rd.

<Callout type="info">You're in — welcome to the pack. Now go chase that high score.</Callout>
</Step>
</Steps>

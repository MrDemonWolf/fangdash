**Effective Date:** March 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how FangDash ("we", "us", "our"), a project of MrDemonWolf, Inc., collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our game and related services at fangdash.mrdemonwolf.workers.dev (the "Service").

<Callout type="info">
	FangDash runs no third-party analytics, ad networks, or tracking pixels — no Google Analytics, no
	gtag, no cross-site advertising cookies. The only data we hold is what you see described below.
</Callout>

## Information We Collect

### Account Information

You can play solo without an account. When you sign in with Twitch OAuth to save progress and race others, we receive and store:

- Your Twitch username (display name)
- Your Twitch user ID (unique identifier)
- Your Twitch profile avatar URL
- Your email address (provided by Twitch during authentication)

We do **not** access your follower list, subscription status, stream data, or any other Twitch data beyond the basic profile fields required for authentication.

### Game Data

When you are signed in, we store your gameplay progress:

- Game scores, distances traveled, and run durations
- Achievement progress and unlock timestamps
- Currently equipped wolf skin and unlocked skins
- Multiplayer race history, results, and placements
- Aggregate stats: total games played, total distance, highest score, and level/XP

Scores are validated server-side by anti-cheat checks. Runs that fail validation are stored but grant no XP, stats, or unlocks.

### Technical Data

We automatically collect standard hosting data through our infrastructure provider, Cloudflare:

- IP address (processed by Cloudflare for security and routing)
- Browser type and version
- Device type and general request metadata
- General geographic region (country level)
- Request timestamps and response times

This is ordinary web-server and CDN log data used to serve the app securely and defend against abuse. We do not build advertising or behavioral profiles from it.

### Cookies and Local Storage

FangDash uses only the storage needed to keep you signed in and remember your preferences:

| Type                  | Purpose                                      | Duration   |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| Authentication cookie | Keeps you signed in (Better Auth session)    | Session    |
| Local storage         | UI preferences, audio volume, tutorial state | Persistent |

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies. Audio settings are stored locally in your browser under the keys `fangdash_volume` and `fangdash_muted`.

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- **Provide the game** — Authenticate your account, save scores, track achievements and skins, and enable multiplayer races
- **Display your identity** — Show your username and avatar on leaderboards and in races
- **Prevent cheating** — Validate score submissions server-side with anti-cheat checks and per-run replay protection
- **Keep the Service healthy** — Review aggregate server metrics from Cloudflare and community feedback to fix bugs and improve performance
- **Communicate** — Respond to support requests via Discord or GitHub

## Third-Party Services

FangDash integrates with the following third-party services:

| Service     | Purpose                                      | Data Shared                                   | Privacy Policy                                                            |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Twitch      | Authentication (OAuth)                       | Handled via OAuth; we receive profile fields  | [Twitch Privacy Notice](https://www.twitch.tv/p/en/legal/privacy-notice/) |
| Cloudflare  | Hosting, CDN, database (D1), DDoS protection | All request data (processed to serve the app) | [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)    |
| PartyKit    | Real-time multiplayer WebSockets             | Player positions and game state during races  | [PartyKit Privacy Policy](https://www.partykit.io/privacy)                |
| Better Auth | Authentication framework                     | Session tokens, user identity (self-hosted)   | Self-hosted — no external data sharing                                    |

## Data Storage and Security

- **Database** — Game data is stored in Cloudflare D1 (SQLite-based), hosted on Cloudflare's global network
- **Authentication** — Sessions are managed by Better Auth, a self-hosted framework running on our Cloudflare Workers
- **Encryption** — All connections to FangDash use HTTPS/TLS encryption
- **Access** — Only authorized team members have access to production data

For more information on Cloudflare's security practices, see the [Cloudflare Trust Hub](https://www.cloudflare.com/trust-hub/).

## Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

FangDash is intended for users aged **13 and older**.

- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
- Signing in requires a Twitch account, and Twitch requires users to be at least 13 years old per their [Terms of Service](https://www.twitch.tv/p/en/legal/terms-of-service/)
- If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can delete it
- We do not target advertising to children or use children's personal information for marketing

## Data Sharing

We do **not** sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal data with third parties for their marketing purposes.

We share data only in these limited circumstances:

- **Twitch** — Authentication only (OAuth token exchange)
- **Cloudflare** — Infrastructure provider (processes requests to serve the application and store data)
- **PartyKit** — Real-time game state during multiplayer races
- **Legal compliance** — We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or a valid government request

## Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your data:

- **Access** — View your profile, scores, achievements, and skins in-game at any time
- **Correction** — Your Twitch username and avatar update automatically when you change them on Twitch
- **Profile visibility** — Toggle your profile between public and private in Settings; private profiles are excluded from public leaderboards
- **Deletion** — Delete your account and data from the in-game Settings page. Deletion has a 24-hour grace period, after which your personal game data is permanently removed. You can also request deletion via Discord
- **Cookie control** — Manage cookies through your browser settings. Note that clearing the authentication cookie will sign you out

### For EEA/UK Residents (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, you also have the right to:

- Request a portable copy of your data
- Object to processing based on legitimate interests
- Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

### For California Residents (CCPA)

California residents have the right to:

- Know what personal information we collect and how it is used
- Request deletion of personal information
- Non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights

We do not sell personal information as defined by the CCPA.

## Data Retention

- **Account data** — Retained for as long as your account exists
- **Game data** (scores, achievements, skins) — Retained for as long as your account exists
- **Server logs** — Retained by Cloudflare per their data-retention policy

When you delete your account, we remove your personally identifiable game data after the 24-hour grace period elapses. Anonymized aggregate data (such as total games played across all players) may be retained for statistical purposes.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes:

- The effective date at the top of this page will be updated
- We may notify you via an in-game notice or on our Discord server

Continued use of FangDash after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

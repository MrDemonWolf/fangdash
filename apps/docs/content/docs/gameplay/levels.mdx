<Callout type="info">
	**TL;DR** — Every point you score is 1 XP. Race podium finishes add a bonus (1st = 500, 2nd = 250,
	3rd = 100). Levels follow a cubic curve, so early levels fly by and later ones reward real
	dedication.
</Callout>

Your **level** is the running total of everything you've earned in FangDash. It climbs as you score, survive, and race — a single number that reflects how much time and skill you've poured into the game.

## How you earn XP

| Source             | XP earned             |
| ------------------ | --------------------- |
| **Score points**   | 1 XP per point scored |
| **1st place race** | 500 bonus XP          |
| **2nd place race** | 250 bonus XP          |
| **3rd place race** | 100 bonus XP          |
| **4th place race** | No bonus XP           |

Every point counts as 1 XP, whether you're playing solo or racing. See [Scoring](/docs/gameplay/scoring) for how points are earned (10 per second survived, 50 per obstacle cleared). Race placements stack a bonus on top of your score.

<Callout type="warn">
	You need to be signed in with Twitch to bank XP. Solo play works without an account, but XP,
	levels, and unlocks only save once you sign in. Cheated runs are stored but grant no XP.
</Callout>

## The level curve

Levels use a **cubic formula**:

```
totalXpForLevel(n) = 5 × (n − 1)³
```

The XP needed for a single level is the gap between two totals: `totalXpForLevel(n) − totalXpForLevel(n − 1)`. Because the curve is cubic, early levels come fast and higher levels take real commitment.

| Level | Total XP needed | XP for this level |
| ----- | --------------- | ----------------- |
| 1     | 0               | —                 |
| 2     | 5               | 5                 |
| 3     | 40              | 35                |
| 5     | 320             | 185               |
| 10    | 3,645           | 1,085             |
| 15    | 13,720          | 2,735             |
| 20    | 34,295          | 5,135             |
| 30    | 121,945         | 12,185            |
| 50    | 588,245         | 35,285            |

## Track your progress

Your current level and XP bar live on your **Profile** page. The bar shows exactly how far you are toward the next level, and it updates automatically after every run.

Check yours anytime at [/profile](/profile), then chase more XP through [multiplayer races](/docs/multiplayer) and higher scores.

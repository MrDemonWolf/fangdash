<Callout type="info">
	**TL;DR** — Your profile shows lifetime stats, your level and XP progress, achievement badges,
	recent score trends, and your equipped wolf skin. Every player gets a public profile, and you
	control its visibility with the privacy toggle in Settings.
</Callout>

Your profile page brings your whole FangDash career together in one view. Visit your own at [/profile](/profile). You need to be signed in with Twitch for stats and XP to be saved — see [Getting Started](/docs/getting-started).

## Performance stats

A stats grid summarizes your lifetime numbers at a glance:

| Stat                  | What it tracks                          |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| **Total Distance**    | Combined meters run across all games    |
| **High Score**        | Your single best score ever             |
| **Win Rate**          | Percentage of races you've won          |
| **Obstacles Cleared** | Total obstacles dodged across all games |
| **Games Played**      | Total number of games completed         |
| **Total Score**       | Combined score across all games         |

These totals only grow from validated runs. Runs flagged by the server's anti-cheat checks are stored but never count toward your stats, XP, or unlocks — see [Scoring](/docs/gameplay/scoring) for how validation works.

## Level & XP

A progress bar shows your current **level** and how far you are from the next one. Every point you score earns 1 XP, and race placements add a bonus: 500 XP for 1st, 250 for 2nd, and 100 for 3rd.

Leveling follows a cubic curve, so each level costs more than the last. See the [Level System](/docs/gameplay/levels) for the full breakdown.

## Achievement badges

Your profile displays a grid of **achievement badges**. Your most recently unlocked achievements show up first, and the remaining slots are filled with locked achievements you haven't earned yet — so you always see a full grid of wins up front and goals still to chase.

There are 21 achievements across five categories (score, distance, games, skill, and social). See [Achievements](/docs/customization/achievements) for the complete list and how to unlock each one.

## Recent score trends

Your most recent scores are listed with **trend arrows** showing whether each run beat the one before it. It's a quick way to see whether you're improving over time or plateauing.

## Equipped skin

Your currently equipped wolf skin is shown on your profile, and other players can see it too. FangDash has six skins, from the default Gray Wolf up to two legendary wolves.

Change your skin anytime from [Settings](/settings), and browse how each one unlocks in [Skins](/docs/customization/skins).

## Public profiles

Every player has a public profile at `/profile/[id]` that other players can view. Public profiles are linked from the leaderboard — click any player's name to open their profile.

A public profile displays:

- Player stats (distance, high score, games played, and more)
- Achievements earned
- Top scores
- Equipped skin

### Share your profile

On your own profile page, a **Share Profile** button copies a direct link to your public profile so you can drop it in chat, a bio, or a stream overlay.

### Privacy toggle

Prefer to stay private? You can turn off public visibility from [Settings](/settings). When your profile is private:

- Your public profile page shows a "This profile is private" notice
- Your name on the leaderboard no longer links to your profile

The toggle is labeled **"Allow others to view my profile"** and can be changed at any time. Your scores can still appear on the leaderboard depending on your settings — the privacy toggle controls access to your profile page. See [Settings](/docs/getting-started/settings) for the full rundown, including account deletion.

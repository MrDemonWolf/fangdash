<Callout type="info">
	**TL;DR** — 5 difficulty tiers, each unlocked by the distance you reach. Speed climbs from 300
	px/s (Easy) toward 800 px/s (Nightmare), obstacles spawn faster, and jumps get heavier.
</Callout>

FangDash uses a **progressive difficulty system**. You always start easy, and the game ramps up automatically the further your wolf runs — there is no difficulty picker.

## Difficulty tiers

Your current tier is chosen by the distance you have reached in the run. Each tier scales speed, spawn frequency, gravity, and which obstacles can appear.

| Distance | Tier      | Color  | Speed × | Spawn × | Gravity × | Max on screen | Obstacle types         |
| -------- | --------- | ------ | ------- | ------- | --------- | ------------- | ---------------------- |
| 0m       | Easy      | Green  | 1.0×    | 1.0×    | 1.0×      | 2             | rock, log, bush        |
| 500m     | Medium    | Yellow | 1.3×    | 1.2×    | 1.0×      | 3             | rock, log, bush, spike |
| 1,500m   | Hard      | Orange | 1.6×    | 1.5×    | 1.1×      | 3             | rock, log, bush, spike |
| 3,000m   | Insane    | Red    | 2.0×    | 1.8×    | 1.15×     | 4             | rock, bush, spike      |
| 5,000m+  | Nightmare | Purple | 2.5×    | 2.2×    | 1.2×      | 5             | bush, spike            |

Base speed is **300 px/s**. The speed multiplier scales your run speed up from there — at Nightmare it targets 750 px/s, and the engine caps top speed at **800 px/s** no matter how far you go.

## What changes as you climb

- **Speed increases** — your wolf runs faster, cutting your reaction time on every obstacle.
- **Gaps narrow** — the spawn multiplier tightens the space between obstacles.
- **Gravity increases** — higher tiers apply a gravity multiplier, so your jumps arc lower and land sooner.
- **More obstacles on screen** — the simultaneous cap grows from 2 (Easy) to 5 (Nightmare).
- **Obstacle mix shifts** — gentler obstacles like the log drop out at the top tiers, leaving only bush and spike at Nightmare.

## Difficulty and your score

Every score is saved with the **tier you reached**. The [leaderboard](/docs/gameplay/leaderboard) lets you filter by difficulty, so your Nightmare runs are ranked against other Nightmare runs — not against someone who never left Easy.

Scoring itself stays the same across tiers: 10 points per second survived plus 50 points per obstacle cleared. See [scoring](/docs/gameplay/scoring) for the full breakdown.

## Tips for high scores

- The first 500 meters are close to free points. Don't take unnecessary risks before Medium unlocks.
- **Medium** is where most players start to struggle — practice reading the tighter gaps here.
- At **Insane and above**, every jump has to be precise. Learn the patterns and use your [double jump](/docs/gameplay/controls) deliberately.

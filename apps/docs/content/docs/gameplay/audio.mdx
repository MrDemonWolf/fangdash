<Callout type="info">
	**TL;DR** — 3 background music tracks switch automatically by context (menu, solo, race), 9 sound
	effects cover every major game event, and your volume and mute settings save to `localStorage` so
	they persist across sessions.
</Callout>

FangDash ships with a full audio system: adaptive background music plus punchy sound effects for the moments that matter, all under one master volume control.

## Background music

Three **BGM tracks** play depending on where you are:

| Track    | When it plays                |
| -------- | ---------------------------- |
| Menu BGM | Main menu and lobby screens  |
| Game BGM | Solo endless runner gameplay |
| Race BGM | Multiplayer race gameplay    |

Music **switches automatically** as you move between screens — the current track crossfades out and the new one takes over, so you never sit in silence between the menu and a run.

## Sound effects

Nine **SFX** fire on the key game events:

| Sound       | Trigger                        |
| ----------- | ------------------------------ |
| Jump        | You jump                       |
| Double jump | You use your second jump       |
| Hit         | You collide with an obstacle   |
| Game over   | Your run ends                  |
| Milestone   | You reach a distance milestone |
| Countdown   | The race countdown ticks       |
| Achievement | You unlock an achievement      |
| Skin equip  | You equip a new skin           |
| Victory     | You win a race                 |

## Volume controls

- **Volume** — a single master slider controls all audio (both music and sound effects). It's stored in `localStorage` under the key `fangdash_volume`.
- **Mute** — a toggle that silences everything at once. It's stored in `localStorage` under the key `fangdash_muted`.

Because both settings live in your browser's `localStorage`, they persist across sessions with no account required. Set your levels once and forget about them.

<Callout type="info">
	Audio settings are stored locally in your browser, not on your FangDash account. If you clear your
	browser data or switch devices, you'll set your volume and mute preferences again.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
	**TL;DR** — Jump with **Space**, or tap/click anywhere. Press again mid-air for a **double jump**.
	**Hold** the jump for a higher leap, release early to cut it short. **Escape** pauses solo runs.
</Callout>

FangDash keeps it simple: one action — jump — mapped to keys, taps, and clicks. Everything else is timing.

## Jump

Jumping is the whole game. You have three ways to do it:

| Input           | Where it works     | Action |
| --------------- | ------------------ | ------ |
| **Space**       | Keyboard           | Jump   |
| **Tap**         | Touchscreen        | Jump   |
| **Mouse click** | Desktop / trackpad | Jump   |

Any tap or click on the game screen triggers a jump, so on desktop you can play with the mouse alone — no keyboard required.

## Double jump

Press jump **again while you're airborne** for a second lift. You get **up to 2 jumps** before you touch the ground again.

Use the double jump to clear taller obstacles, stretch over a gap, or recover from a first jump you mistimed.

## Variable jump height

Your jump height depends on how long you hold the button:

- **Hold** Space (or keep tapping held) to jump **higher**.
- **Release early** to **cut the jump short** and drop back down fast.

This gives you fine control over your arc. Tap briefly to hop small obstacles, hold to soar over the big ones. Mastering short hops keeps you low and ready for whatever spawns next.

## Pause

Press **Escape** to open the pause menu during a solo run. From there you can resume or leave the run.

<Callout type="warn">
	Pause is a solo-play feature. Races run in real time on a shared course, so there's no pausing
	mid-race — see [How races work](/docs/multiplayer/how-it-works) for the details.
</Callout>

## Debug panel

**Ctrl + Shift + D** opens an in-game debug panel. It's restricted to developer and admin accounts and isn't part of normal play — you can safely ignore it.

## Controller support

Controllers are **not supported yet**. FangDash is built for keyboard, touch, and mouse. Gamepad support is an idea we may explore later, but it isn't available today and isn't promised for any release.

<Callout type="info">
	**TL;DR** — FangDash has 6 wolf skins across 5 rarity tiers. Gray Wolf is the default; the other 5
	are earned. Every unlock is checked against your **best single run**, and three skins double as
	achievement rewards.
</Callout>

FangDash ships with **6 wolf skins** spread across 5 rarity tiers. You start with the Gray Wolf and earn the rest by hitting distance, score, and achievement milestones.

<Callout type="warn">
	Skin unlocks compare against your **best single run**, not your lifetime totals. A skin that asks
	for 5,000 points wants 5,000 in one run — spreading it across sessions won't do it.
</Callout>

## All skins

### Gray Wolf — Common

![Gray Wolf](/wolves/wolf-gray.png)

**Default.** Available from the start, no unlock required. This is your starter wolf.

---

### Shadow Wolf — Uncommon

![Shadow Wolf](/wolves/wolf-shadow.png)

Reach **2,000m** in a single run.

---

### Fire Wolf — Rare

![Fire Wolf](/wolves/wolf-fire.png)

Score **5,000 points** in a single run. You also earn this skin the moment you unlock the **Marathon Runner** achievement (run 5,000m in a single run) — whichever comes first.

---

### Storm Wolf — Epic

![Storm Wolf](/wolves/wolf-storm.png)

Unlock the **Obstacle Master** achievement (clear 1,000 obstacles total). The skin is the achievement's reward.

---

### Blood Moon Wolf — Legendary

![Blood Moon Wolf](/wolves/wolf-blood-moon.png)

Score **15,000 points** in a single run.

---

### MrDemonWolf — Legendary

![MrDemonWolf](/wolves/wolf-mrdemonwolf.png)

Unlock the **Champion** achievement (win 10 races). The skin is the achievement's reward.

## Rarity tiers

| Tier      | Skins                        |
| --------- | ---------------------------- |
| Common    | Gray Wolf                    |
| Uncommon  | Shadow Wolf                  |
| Rare      | Fire Wolf                    |
| Epic      | Storm Wolf                   |
| Legendary | Blood Moon Wolf, MrDemonWolf |

Fire Wolf, Storm Wolf, and MrDemonWolf are also tied to achievements, so chasing those unlocks earns you the skin at the same time. See [Achievements](/docs/customization/achievements) for the full list.

## Browsing skins

The [Skins gallery](/skins) is a **view-only** page where you can preview every wolf and read its unlock condition. Unlocked skins are highlighted; locked ones show exactly what you need to earn them.

## Equipping skins

Equipping happens on the **Settings** page.

<Steps>
	<Step>Browse the [Skins gallery](/skins) to see what's available.</Step>
	<Step>Open [Settings](/settings) to reach the skin picker.</Step>
	<Step>Select any unlocked skin to equip it.</Step>
</Steps>

Your equipped skin shows up to other players in multiplayer races and on your public profile.

See [Settings](/docs/getting-started/settings) for more details.

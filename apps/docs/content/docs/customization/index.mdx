FangDash gives you unlockable **wolf skins** and **achievements** so there's always a next thing to chase. Sign in with Twitch to save your progress — skins, achievements, and XP all persist to your account.

## Wolf skins

There are **six wolf skins** in total. **Gray Wolf** is your free starter, and the other five unlock through gameplay milestones and achievements. Each skin has its own look and rarity: common, uncommon, rare, epic, and legendary.

Unlocks are checked against your **best single run**, so a strong session can pop a new skin the moment you beat its threshold. Browse and equip skins from the [Skins gallery](/skins) in the web app.

## Achievements

**21 achievements** across 5 categories challenge you to master every part of the game. Earn them by hitting score milestones, covering long distances, playing lots of games, pulling off skillful moves, and racing other wolves.

Three achievements reward **exclusive skins** on unlock — Marathon Runner grants the Fire Wolf, Obstacle Master grants the Storm Wolf, and Champion grants the MrDemonWolf skin. Check your progress anytime on the [Achievements page](/achievements).

## Level & XP

Every point you score earns **1 XP** toward your player level. Finishing on the podium in a race adds bonus XP: 1st = 500, 2nd = 250, 3rd = 100. Your level and progress bar live on your [Profile](/profile).
